# Code of conduct

Help make this project welcoming and useful to people with different backgrounds
and experience levels. Use respectful language, discuss the work rather than the
person, and give specific, constructive feedback. English and Vietnamese are both
welcome.

Harassment, discriminatory language, threats, unwanted contact, and publishing
another person's private information are not acceptable. Respect boundaries and
stop behavior when asked.

Maintainers may edit or remove content, close discussions, or restrict participation
to address harmful behavior. Decisions should be proportionate, protect affected
people's privacy, and explain the relevant rule when possible.

To report a problem, contact a maintainer through the contact methods on their
GitHub profile. Do not post private information in a public issue. If the report
concerns a maintainer, contact another maintainer where one is available.
